Roof flood prevention

Flood prevention keeps your flat roof clear of anything that blocks drainage: leaves, debris, clogged drains. The goal is simple, water leaves instead of pooling.

Get a free quote (514) 993-6897

Why it matters

Standing water is a flat roof's worst enemy. It wears the membrane faster, freezes in winter, and seeps through the smallest seam. We remove the causes of pooling before they get expensive.

What the service includes

  • Inspecting low spots and drains
  • Removing what blocks the flow
  • Checking flashing and seams
  • A preventive maintenance plan
