Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to what we get asked most. Don't see your question? Give us a call.

How often should a flat roof in Montréal be cleaned?

Usually once or twice a year: in the fall after the leaves drop, and in spring after the melt. A roof surrounded by trees or prone to debris may need more. We can set a schedule around your roof.

How much does roof maintenance or snow removal cost?

It depends on the roof's size, access, and condition. We don't quote off the top of our head: we assess on-site for free and give you a clear price before we start, no surprises.

When should you remove snow from your roof?

When snow builds up enough to add real weight, especially after a big storm or freezing rain. On a flat roof we also watch for ice blocking drainage. When in doubt, call and we assess.

Are you insured?

Yes. Toit à Toit Entretien carries full liability insurance. You are covered while we work on your roof.

Are your methods safe for my roof?

Yes. We work by hand and avoid anything that could damage the membrane or gravel. The goal is to extend your roof’s life, not wear it down.

Which areas do you serve?

Montréal and surrounding areas: Saint-Léonard, Ahuntsic-Cartierville, Le Plateau-Mont-Royal, Montréal-Nord, Laval, Saint-Bruno, and Brossard. Not sure about your area? Just ask.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes, our work is guaranteed. If something is not right after we leave, we come back.
