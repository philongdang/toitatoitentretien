About us

We tell you the truth about your roof, even when it costs us the job.

Toit à Toit Entretien is honest, local, insured roof care on the Island of Montréal. Our job: keep roofs clean and safe, and tell you straight what yours actually needs.

Our approach

Behind Toit à Toit Entretien is Richard Gontarski and a simple idea: in this trade, trust matters more than polish. We don't invent problems, we don't sell you what you don't need, and we treat every roof as if it were our own.

A well-maintained roof lasts longer. It is less an expense than an investment in your roof’s lifespan, and that is how we approach it.
