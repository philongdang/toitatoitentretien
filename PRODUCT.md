# PRODUCT — Toit à Toit Entretien

**Register: brand / marketing** (design IS the product — this is a lead-gen site).

## What it is
A conversion-first, bilingual (FR canonical / EN mirror) lead-generation site for a
Montréal roof-cleaning and roof-maintenance company. Not a showcase — every screen
exists to turn a homeowner who searched "nettoyage toiture Montréal" into a phone
call or a quote request.

## Subject
Honest, local, insured roof care in Montréal's harsh seasonal climate. The world of
the subject: slate and membrane roofs, copper flashing, roof drains, autumn leaves,
snow load, the Montréal winter sky, ladders, the seasonal maintenance cycle.

## Audience
Montréal homeowners and small property managers. Often stressed, often on mobile,
usually comparing 2–3 companies. They care about: is my roof at risk, are you insured,
what will it cost, can I reach a human now, are you actually local.

## The one job of every page
Get a phone call (`tel:+15149936897`) or a free-quote request ("Soumission gratuite").

## Differentiator (lead with this)
**Honesty.** The company once gave a customer a free estimate and told her to wait a
year before cleaning because she didn't need it yet. "We tell you the truth about your
roof, even when it costs us the job." Trust converts in trades, not gloss.

## Voice
Plain, confident, local, reassuring. Québécois French (not France French). Active
voice, sentence case, no corporate filler. "Demandez une soumission gratuite," not
"Soumettre une demande."

## Hard constraints
- Phone tap-to-call + free-quote CTA reachable within one tap from any page.
- Core Web Vitals all "Good" on mobile; nothing blocks first paint.
- WCAG 2.1 AA; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected; tap targets ≥ 44px.
- Real `réalisations` photos only — **no AI-generated or faked job photos** (it would
  poison the honesty brand). Placeholders here are clearly labelled as samples.
- No invented business facts — unverified claims live in `TODO-VERIFY.md`.
